About Legin' Abroad

I turn real development problems into practical guides.

I'm Alex, a Python developer and software tester with a background in physics. I write about the work behind reliable applications: implementation, automated testing, deployment, and the lessons that only appear after software meets the real world.

The story

Curiosity, code, and life abroad.

I started in physics, where asking precise questions and testing assumptions is part of the job. That way of thinking followed me into software development and quality engineering. Today, I use Python to build applications, automate repetitive work, and make systems easier to trust.

The name Legin' Abroad comes from years of living outside my home country—including student life in the Czech Republic and almost a year in Japan. The site began as a place to record those experiences. It has grown into a focused technical notebook for the problems I solve and the tools I use.

Travel will have its own place here, too. A dedicated Travel Notes section is planned for stories and practical observations from the Czech Republic, Japan, and future journeys.

My goal is simple: publish the explanation I wish I had when I first faced the problem.
