About Legin' Abroad

Meet Alex, the writer behind Legin’ Abroad

I’m a Python developer and software tester with a background in physics. I started Legin’ Abroad to share what I learn from building projects and making a life outside my home country.

Explore the articles See my GitHub

The story

Curiosity, code, and life abroad.

I started in physics, where asking precise questions and testing assumptions is part of the job. That way of thinking followed me into software development and quality engineering. Today, I use Python to build applications, automate repetitive work, and make systems easier to trust.

The name Legin' Abroad comes from years of living outside my home country. Adapting to unfamiliar places taught me to observe carefully, question assumptions, and document the details that make complicated systems easier to understand. That perspective shapes the way I write about software, life abroad, and other subjects I explore through hands-on work or research.

My goal is simple: publish the explanation I wish I had when I first faced the problem.
