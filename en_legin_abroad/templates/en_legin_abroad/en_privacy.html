Privacy and analytics settings

Visiting Legin' Abroad

When your browser requests a page or file, the servers delivering it receive request details such as your IP address, the URL and your browser type. This information helps deliver the site, diagnose errors and protect it from abuse. The site may also use essential cookies for sessions and security.

Pages request fonts from Google Fonts. Some articles embed content such as YouTube videos. The providers receive requests from your browser when those resources load and may use cookies or similar technologies. Their privacy policies govern their handling of that information.

If you enable analytics, Google Analytics uses cookies and identifiers to help measure page visits and interactions. Google can receive information such as the pages you visit, your browser and device, and your IP address as part of the request. This helps Alex understand which guides readers use. The site stores your choice in this browser. You can turn analytics off here at any time or clear site data in your browser to reset your choice.
